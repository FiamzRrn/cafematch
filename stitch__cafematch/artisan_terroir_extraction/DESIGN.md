## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the meticulous precision, warmth, and sensory depth of third-wave specialty coffee. Tailored for coffee sommeliers, roasters, and discerning home brewers, the interface positions expert cupping recommendations alongside editorial storytelling. The tone avoids cold SaaS aesthetics and rustic café tropes in favor of an understated, tactile editorial luxury reminiscent of independent culinary quarterlies (such as *Standart* or *Drift*).

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Open breathing room, structured typographic scale, and natural material palettes inspired by linen, raw ceramic, and slow-pour extraction.
- **Sensory Tactility:** Low-contrast hairline dividers, subtle textural shifts from oat-milk canvases to steamed-porcelain surfaces, and restrained micro-interactions that mirror careful craft.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Strict column alignments paired with graceful serif display headlines to convey authority, traceability, and artisanal expertise.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the physical realities of specialty coffee harvesting, roasting, and brewing:

- **Primary (`#1A1412` - Roasted Espresso):** Used for commanding headlines, high-emphasis text, structural framing, and primary action controls. Provides pitch-black depth without the sterility of pure `#000000`.
- **Secondary (`#C86D51` / Deep Variant `#9E472A` - Roasted Terracotta & Amber):** Represents the Maillard reaction and slow extraction. Used sparingly for curated recommendation highlights, cupping score accents, active tab indicators, and contextual CTAs.
- **Tertiary (`#5C6B5E` - Olive Processing Wash):** A muted botanical sage used for origin elevation badges, agricultural processing tags (e.g., Anaerobic Natural, Washed), and organic notes.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5` - Steamed Oat Linen):** The base background wash. Replaces clinical white with a calm, organic warmth.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#F4EFEA` - Raw Ceramic):** Container fill for cards, search bars, and floating cupping panels.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#EDE7DF` - Crema Tint):** Secondary container fill for inset groupings, interactive item hover states, and recipe steps.
- **Borders & Hairlines (`#E6DDD2`):** Ultra-fine neutral borders delivering architectural definition without visual noise.

## Typography

The type scale balances high-contrast editorial elegance with utilitarian clarity:

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys the literary gravitas of an authoritative coffee journal. Used for bean origins, varietal titles, brew method features, and expert cupper names. Maintain italicized variants strictly for emotional cues, tasting notes (e.g., *Bergamot, Jasmine, Candied Peach*), or regional heritage markers.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides geometric crispness and high legibility at dense scales, such as grind sizes, water-to-coffee ratios, cupping scores, and filter parameters.
- **Uppercase Labels (`label-caps`):** Rendered with deliberate tracking (`0.08em`) to act as category signposts, processing method indicators, and metadata keys.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial grid with deliberate asymmetrical white space:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Feature articles and origin reviews use offset 7-column editorial spans flanked by 3-column sticky cupping summary cards.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Sticky sidebars collapse into persistent horizontal carousels or bottom-anchored sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Full bleed imagery transitions with inset content blocks, keeping card padding disciplined at `1rem`.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to multiples of `0.5rem` (`8px`), with generous macro-spacing between sections (`4rem` to `6rem`) to maintain an unhurried, thoughtful pace.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, artificial blurs, and plastic skeuomorphism. Depth is established through structural layering and tactile contrast:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Layering rests on pure tone shifts rather than drop shadows. Base canvas (`#FBF9F5`) holds the first level cards (`#F4EFEA`), which contain secondary groupings or interactive cells (`#EDE7DF`).
- **Hairline Framing:** Cards, dropdowns, and separated lists utilize a uniform `1px` border in `#E6DDD2`. This imparts the structure of fine editorial print.
- **Ambient Contact Shadow (Floating Elements Only):** For floating filter bars, recommendation drawers, or active dialogue modals, depth is achieved through an extremely soft, warm-tinted shadow: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.08)`. Hover states on cards do not elevate; they shift border contrast to `#C86D51` or deepen internal surface warmth.

## Shapes

The design system combines architectural precision with soft pill accents:

- **Containers, Cards, & Windows:** Set to `roundedness: 1` (`4px` / `0.25rem` up to `8px` / `0.5rem` on large feature containers). This preserves clean horizontal and vertical lines characteristic of a printed magazine.
- **Badges, Tags, & Pills:** Fully rounded capsule/pill shapes (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for metadata badges (e.g., roast level, processing method, Q-Grader score) to visually distinguish fluid tags from structured content boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Dark Roasted Espresso (`#1A1412`) background with Oat Milk (`#FBF9F5`) text. Subtle `4px` border radius. On hover, background deepens to `#0D0A09` with a subtle `2px` horizontal push.
- **Secondary / Terroir:** Bordered with `1px solid #1A1412`, transparent background, dark espresso text. On hover, fills with Terracotta Amber (`#C86D51`) and shifts text to white.
- **Ghost:** Minimal text button in `#1A1412` or `#C86D51` paired with a small directional glyph (e.g., `->`), accompanied by a persistent hairline underline with `4px` offset.

### Chips & Flavor Badges
- **Cupping Notes Chip:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#EDE7DF` background, `#1A1412` text, `0.75rem` vertical padding, `1.25rem` horizontal padding. Font set to `label-sm`.
- **Origin / Process Badge:** Pill-shaped, Sage tint (`#5C6B5E` at 12% opacity) background with `#3D483E` text, accompanied by an uppercase tracking style (`label-caps`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Flat `#F4EFEA` background, bottom hairline border `1px solid #C4B8AA`. Focus removes generic focus rings and sharpens the bottom border to a `1.5px solid #1A1412` line. Placeholder text sits in muted roasted crema (`#9E9388`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unchecked states feature an unadorned `1.5px solid #C4B8AA` square or circle on `#FBF9F5`. Checked state fills smoothly with `#1A1412` hosting a crisp white checkmark.

### Cards & Cupping Profiles
- **Coffee Recommendation Card:** Background `#F4EFEA`, border `1px solid #E6DDD2`, `8px` corner radius. Visual presentation leads with a structured header (Varietal, Farm/Woreda, Roaster), followed by the cupper's sensory score (styled in `display-hero` scale terracotta text), and concludes with a footer containing extraction recipe toggles (V60, Aeropress, Kalita).
- **Sensory Radar / Spectrum Bar:** Continuous low-contrast background track (`#EDE7DF`) with a warm terracotta slider indicator showing Acidity, Body, Sweetness, and Clarity.

### Sensory Data Tables & Lists
- Thin horizontal lines (`#E6DDD2`) separate line items with generous vertical padding (`1.25rem`). Columns balance left-aligned descriptive metadata in `body-sm` with right-aligned numeric technical specs (e.g., Altitude: `1,950 - 2,100 MASL`) in mono-spaced clarity.
